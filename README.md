# INK RALLY

Draw a side-view silhouette, then race the paper cutout.

The right edge of the page is the nose. Long, light, pointy ink finds top speed. A heavy flat belly stays planted. Jagged feet and a tall body want to slide. Nothing here is borrowed from another racer — the shapes, names, and tracks are original.

## Play

- **Draw** with pen, eraser, and fill. Fill only stays inside a closed shape.
- **Race** on Quill Circuit, Blotter Bend, or Margin Marathon for 3, 5, or 8 laps.
- **Steer** with arrows or A/D. **Space** drifts and fills the boost vial. **Shift** spends it. **S** brakes.
- Red speed means the next bend is tighter than your line.
- Green strips on the paper are boost pads.
- **Time trial** races the ghost of your best run on that page.
- **Ink Cup** is three rounds, same ink, points 10–7–5–3–2–1.
- Drawings, bests, ghosts, and the cup stay in this browser. A share link restores the silhouette.
