# SKETCH SEND

Draw a side-view silhouette, then send the paper cutout. The repo stays `ink-rally`; the night race is SKETCH SEND.

The right edge of the page is the nose. Long, light, pointy ink finds top speed. A heavy flat belly stays planted. Jagged feet and a tall body want to slide. Nothing here is borrowed from another racer — the shapes, names, and tracks are original.

## Play

- **Sketch** with pen, eraser, and fill. Fill only stays inside a closed shape.
- **Send it** on Pop Shuvit, Curb Check, or Hill Bomb for 3, 5, or 8 laps.
- **Steer** with arrows or A/D. **Space** drifts and fills the SEND vial. **Shift** spends it. **S** brakes.
- Red speed means the next bend is tighter than your line.
- Lime gates on the paper are SEND pads.
- Purple **ink slicks** spin you. Orange **sticky blots** glue the wheels. **Cone rails** bounce you. **Page sweepers** cross the lane.
- Stands and a paper crowd line both sides of every circuit.
- **Ghost Lap** races the ghost of your best run on that circuit.
- **Pack Heat** and **Full Grid** are the live fields. **Smear Cup** is three rounds, same sled, points 10–7–5–3–2–1.
- Drawings, bests, ghosts, and the cup stay in this browser. A send link restores the silhouette.
